# FARMDIARIES

> Enterprise-grade smart agriculture and vision-powered farming platform for Vietnamese smallholder farmers.

FarmDiaries is a mobile-first smart farming diary system that helps farmers record crop activities, follow care schedules, receive AI-assisted farming support, and build a reusable history of crop seasons.

## Project Overview

FARMDIARIES focuses on digital transformation in agriculture. The system is designed for smallholder crop farmers in Vietnam who need a practical way to monitor farming activities, manage crop-care schedules, detect possible disease risks earlier, and review farming history across seasons.

The project addresses common problems in traditional farm management:

- Farmers often rely on memory or handwritten notebooks to manage crop activities.
- Important care tasks such as watering, fertilizing, or pesticide spraying can be missed.
- Crop diseases may be detected too late because there is no early digital support mechanism.
- Historical season data is usually fragmented, making it difficult to compare results and improve future farming decisions.
- Agricultural advice is often generic instead of being adapted to the farmer's crop, history, and current conditions.

## Project Topic

The topic of FARMDIARIES is a smart agriculture diary platform that combines:

- Digital farm diary management.
- AI-assisted farming recommendations.
- Personalized crop-care reminders.
- Vision-powered plant disease support.
- Weekly farming summaries.
- Farmer engagement through a virtual pet feature.
- Administrative management of users and crop data.

In the Software Requirements Specification, the project is described as an enterprise-grade smart agriculture and vision-powered farming platform. Its main goal is to create an intelligent mobile diary that can record, digitize, and store the complete chronology of a farm in a more automatic and scientific way.

## Core Features

### 1. Smart Farm Diary

Farmers can create, update, and manage farming records for each crop season. A diary entry can capture important farming details such as crop type, growth stage, watering, fertilizing, pesticide spraying, disease-prevention activity, and care notes.

### 2. AI Assistant

The AI Assistant acts as a virtual agricultural companion. It helps farmers ask farming questions, receive practical suggestions, and get contextual support based on crop information and historical farming activity.

### 3. Personalized Care Reminders

The system analyzes crop information, farming history, and current conditions to provide reminders for important care tasks. This reduces the chance of missing key farming stages such as watering, fertilizing, or pesticide spraying.

### 4. Vision-Powered Disease Support

FarmDiaries includes the concept of AI-based leaf-image scanning. Farmers can capture plant leaves and receive support for identifying possible crop disease signs or early warnings.

### 5. Weekly Farming Reports

The system provides weekly summaries so farmers can review what happened during the week, understand completed activities, and recognize what needs attention.

### 6. Virtual Pet Engagement

The virtual pet feature encourages regular platform usage and makes the farming diary experience more engaging. It supports farmer interaction beyond simple data entry.

### 7. AI Quality Rating

Farmers can rate the quality of AI responses and recommendations. This feedback supports future improvement of the assistant and helps evaluate whether the advice is useful in real farming contexts.

## Expected Impact

FARMDIARIES aims to help farmers move from manual, fragmented record-keeping to a more organized, intelligent, and data-supported farming workflow.

Expected benefits include:

- Better tracking of crop activities.
- Fewer missed care tasks.
- Earlier awareness of possible crop diseases.
- More useful historical data for comparing crop seasons.
- More personalized farming advice.
- Improved farmer engagement with digital agriculture tools.
- Stronger support for sustainable and efficient crop management.
